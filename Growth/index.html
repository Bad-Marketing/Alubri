ALUBRI Amazon Growth Strategy

Prepared by FTR  ·  Amazon & Walmart Growth

Growth Goal: Increase monthly Amazon revenue from approximately $15K to $25K.

Summary

Alubri has grown from $4.4K in February to nearly $15K in September 2026. However, sales have remained flat since July, while advertising spend increased 39% from August to September.

Alubri has a strong product offering and clinical credibility, but its current Amazon presence does not communicate that value effectively. The priority is to improve listings, grow full-size sales, and make it easier for customers to discover and purchase products across the catalog.

Key Opportunities
  • Lead with clinical credibility: Make Alubri's OB/GYN co-founder, scientific expertise, and approved clinical evidence central to Amazon positioning.
  • Improve listing performance: Optimize SEO, main images, image stacks, and Premium A+ Content to improve visibility and conversion.
  • Grow full-size sales: Minis accounted for 28% of September units but only 16% of revenue. Create a stronger path from trial to full-size purchases.
  • Grow non-branded sales: Improve visibility and click share across relevant generic searches.
  • Connect the catalog: Use A+ Content, Brand Story, and the Amazon Store to introduce complementary products and encourage additional purchases. (Customer Journey)
  • Increase repeat purchases: Build on 162 existing Subscribe & Save subscriptions, prioritizing full-size Gel and Balm.
  • Build trust and awareness: Increase reviews and get more value from Creator Connections.

Strategic Priority: Improve the performance of the existing catalog, with a focus on conversion, full-size sales, and repeat purchases before expanding the product offering.

Growth Area 1: Listing Optimization & Conversion

Objective: Improve search visibility, click-through rates, and conversion through stronger SEO, clinical positioning, and listing creative.

Opportunity

Current main images show products too small, with limited packaging visibility and inconsistent quality. Image stacks and A+ Content also need clearer product education and a more premium presentation.

September Search Query Performance shows that Alubri is losing clicks on relevant generic searches:

Vulvar moisturizer1.80%0.20%
Vulva moisturizer2.16%0.72%
Key Recommendations
Clinical Positioning
  • Make Alubri's OB/GYN co-founder, medical expertise, and approved clinical evidence central to its Amazon messaging.
  • Move away from playful intimate care messaging and focus on clinical credibility, product science, and clear benefits.
  • Keep the messaging warm and approachable while maintaining a premium feel across listings, Brand Story, and the Store.
Listing SEO
  • Fully optimize titles, bullets, descriptions, backend keywords, and product attributes using search data and customer intent.
  • Clearly communicate product benefits, usage, and differences between products, using Amazon-compliant claims.
Main Images & Image Stacks
  • Redesign main images with larger products, readable packaging, and high-quality photography or renders.
  • Create consistent, mobile-first image stacks with clear benefits, clinical evidence, and relevant lifestyle imagery.
  • Use premium, science-focused brands such as Prequel, Seed, and Function Health as creative references.
Premium A+ Content
  • Develop mobile-first Premium A+ Content focused on product education, clinical credibility, and clear benefits.
  • Include product comparisons and recommendations for complementary products.
Product Benefits & Consistent Use
  • Explain product benefits and why consistent use matters.
  • Where supported by clinical evidence, communicate when customers can expect to see results.
  • Show how products fit into daily care routines through lifestyle imagery and messaging.

Priority: Start with full-size Vulva Balm and Vaginal Moisturizing Gel, which together account for approximately 75% of sales.

Growth Area 2: Customer Journey & Catalog Growth

Objective: Improve product discovery, connect complementary products, and convert mini buyers into full-size customers.

Opportunity

Mini sales increased from 34 to 197 units between August and September, making them an important entry point. However, the current Amazon experience does little to guide customers toward full-size products or introduce complementary products.

Key Recommendations
Mini-to-Full-Size Journey
  • Position minis for trial and full-size products for regular use.
  • Clearly communicate the benefits and value of full-size formats.
  • Connect minis to corresponding full-size products through A+ Content and Store navigation.
Complementary Product Discovery
  • Clearly explain the differences between Gel, Balm, Serum, and Calming Cream.
  • Use product comparisons and cross-selling modules to introduce complementary products and relevant care routines.
  • Connect products across A+ Content, Brand Story, and the Store.
Amazon Store
  • Build a premium, mobile-first Store with clear product navigation.
  • Organize products around customer needs and complementary care routines.
  • Highlight the founder story, clinical credibility, and full catalog.
Growth Area 3: Subscribe & Save & Retention

Objective: Grow full-size subscriptions and recurring revenue.

Opportunity

Alubri has approximately 162 active subscriptions, with 75% concentrated in full-size Gel (61) and Balm (60).

The existing 20% first-order Subscribe & Save coupon is performing well, generating $2,158.59 in sales, 131 units sold, and a 17.5% conversion rate in the provided performance snapshot.

Key Recommendations
Grow Full-Size Subscriptions
  • Prioritize Gel and Balm, with additional opportunities across Serum and Calming Cream.
  • Highlight the benefits of consistent use and convenient replenishment.
  • Maintain the existing 20% first-order coupon and 10% Subscribe & Save discount where applicable.
Review Mini Enrollment
  • Consider removing Subscribe & Save from minis after reviewing existing subscriptions.
  • Keep minis focused on trial and encourage customers to subscribe to full-size products.
Improve Retention
  • Review subscription growth, cancellations, delivery frequency, and recurring revenue once dashboard access is restored.
  • Monitor coupon profitability and subscription performance before making changes.

Creative Direction

Existing branding is strong - we’ll be following this closely to ensure the brand is instantly recognizable across all platforms, while optimizing specifically for conversion on Amazon.

The brand book voice is witty and provocative (“standing O,” “future is fluids”). The Amazon growth strategy calls for clinical credibility.

Recommendation:
  • Amazon conversion surfaces (main image, images 2–7, product A+): about 80% Scientific / Approachable and 20% Confident
  • Brand Story and Store: room for one or two Witty headlines (“Dryness isn’t destiny,” “Evidence is our love language”)
  • Innuendo lines: stay on social

This keeps the brand recognizable. It also respects how Amazon shoppers actually decide: comparing proof side by side in about 10 seconds on a phone.

USP hierarchy for Amazon (in order of what to lead with)
  1. Developed by a menopause-certified OB/GYN. Rare in this competitive set. Use the founder’s face, name, and credentials.
  2. Clinically studied, microbiome-supporting formula (proprietary Activated Oil Complex: coconut + rosehip). This is the true point of difference vs. Replens, Medicine Mama, and Good Clean Love. Visualize the data.
  3. Hormone-free + fragrance-free. The deciding filter for estrogen-avoidant and sensitive shoppers. Fragrance-free directly beats Vagisil and many “natural” scented options.
  4. Skincare-grade feel and design. Non-greasy texture, display-worthy packaging. Answers the “messy and medicinal” objection.
  5. Tested beyond cosmetic standards. Gyn and derm tested, HRIPT on 200 people, hypoallergenic, in-vitro irritation testing.

On duration: “Up to 72 hours of hydration” is a strong Gel benefit, but Replens makes a similar multi-day claim. Always stack it with #2 and #3 so it reads as “hydration plus microbiome health,” not a parity claim.

Search results → click (fixing the CTR problem)

SQP shows click share at roughly a third of impression share on “vulva balm” and “vulvar moisturizer.” This is the first job for creative.

  • Main images, all SKUs - we need to improve the quality displayed of the product and box
    • Product plus carton filling about 85% of the frame
    • Label facing forward, product name readable at thumbnail size
    • Consistent angle across the catalog
    • Balm: jar plus carton with the teal lid’s highlight visible
    • Gel, Cream, and Serum: tube in front of the carton
Image stack logic (the PDP buying journey)

Each image needs to answer the following questions: What is it? → Does it work? → Who says so? → Is it safe? → How do I use it? → What else do I need?

Premium A+ Content (product-level), mobile-first module order

It’s imperative that we optimize first for mobile - we can see some premium A+ content starting to roll out across listings, but we need to ensure all listings have this in order to be poised for mobile-first shopping.

The existing A+ content is disjointed - we’ll be looking to bring cohesion and carry the branding effectively throughout all modules to create a more pleasurable experience for the consumer.

Brand Story and Store

Hone in on sisterhood, the origins of the brand and background. This is where the witty voice can surface once, e.g., “Dryness isn’t destiny.” This is where we’ll focus on cross-selling and ensuring customers are guided through their buying journey.

Advertising

Objective: Reduce wasted ad spend, bring ACOS down (100%), and use advertising to grow sales and organic rank.

Opportunity

In September, Alubri spent $11.2K on ads for $6.7K in ad sales (167% ACOS). A budgeting tool was changing daily budgets every few days. It gave the most budget to the weakest campaigns and limited the strongest ones to one or two clicks a day.

Progress to date: The budget automation was paused on October 5. Since then:

  • Budgets have been reset to the October split.
  • Campaigns are organized into portfolios by product group.
  • New low bid and keyword targeted campaigns have launched using Data Dive research.
  • During Prime Big Deal Days, ACOS dropped by nearly 50 percentage points and conversion rate reached 19.4%.
Key Recommendations
Campaign Structure & Budget Control
  • Manage spend through bids and placements rather than frequent budget changes.
  • Keep campaigns organized by product group for clearer reporting and decision making.
Reduce Wasted Spend
  • Lower bids on auto and discovery campaigns
  • Move spend away from low converting placements.
Targeted Keyword Campaigns (Done for SP)
  • Build exact match campaigns around the highest intent terms from the new Data Dive research for each product group.
  • Increase bids gradually on keywords that convert, and isolate the strongest into dedicated campaigns.
Minis & Full Sizes
  • Use minis to compete with lower priced competitors, and full sizes to compete with premium competitors where Alubri offers better value.
Sponsored Brands & Competitor Targeting
  • Expand Sponsored Brands video and headline campaigns to the keywords and products that perform best in Sponsored Products.
  • Test competitor brand and product targeting focusing on competitors where Alubri wins on price or format.
